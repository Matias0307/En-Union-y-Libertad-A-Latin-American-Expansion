# En Unión y Libertad+ | A Latin American Expansion

A Hearts of Iron IV mod that brings major-nation-level depth to Latin America — unique focus trees, custom leaders, voice acting, and new art assets for countries the base game largely overlooks.

[![Steam Workshop](https://img.shields.io/badge/Steam-Workshop-1b2838?logo=steam)](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3485554076)

## Overview

*En Unión y Libertad+* expands South and Central America into a fully realized theater for Hearts of Iron IV, with the same level of detail players expect from majors like Germany or the Soviet Union. The project is built and maintained by a seven-person team and has surpassed 40,000 subscribers on the Steam Workshop.

## Supported Nations

Unique focus trees and enhanced content for:

- Brazil
- Argentina
- Chile
- Uruguay
- Peru
- Bolivia
- Paraguay
- Colombia
- Venezuela
- Ecuador
- Central American nations (more in development)

## Features

- Fully unique national focus trees for every major nation listed above
- Custom national ideas, advisors, and leader characters for all South American countries
- New state divisions and territorial adjustments
- Voice acting for most Latin American nations
- Unique 3D soldier models, with a special focus on Argentine forces
- Custom 2D/3D aircraft and tank models

## Installation

1. Subscribe to the mod on the [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3485554076).
2. Launch Hearts of Iron IV and enable **En Unión y Libertad+** from the in-game mod launcher.
3. Start or load a game as one of the supported nations.

## Compatibility

- **DLC required:** None — all DLC is supported, none is mandatory
- **Incompatible with:** Road to 56, Kaiserreich, Millennium Dawn, and other major overhaul/total-conversion mods
- **Languages:** English, Español, Português (community translations available for Russian, Chinese, and Korean)
